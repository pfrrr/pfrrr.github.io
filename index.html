About me

Hello, dear friend!

I worked as a full-stack developer in custom development for 15 years. At one point in my life, I decided to dedicate myself entirely to game creation.

I've always sought new challenges, and at this point in my life, game development has become attractive to me.

My position in life is that a person should never stop learning. They should seek out new challenges, overcome them, and move forward. Knowledge broadens horizons, develops understanding, humility, and empathy.

I'm against AI doing human creative work. Creation is a process of absorption. This goes against my principles.

I don't use AI in my games, and I write my code myself. I even wrote this website myself.

I hope you will like my work!

Sincerely, pfrrr
